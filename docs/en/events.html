How it works

Three steps, no worries

  1. 01

    You tell us

    The date, the place, the number of guests: we suggest a menu and a quote.

  2. 02

    The fire does its work

    The meat smokes for 10 to 12 hours in our ovens, then travels to you, ready to serve.

  3. 03

    Carved on site

    Our team carves the meat in front of your guests, right as it is served. The show is part of the meal.

We travel across Greater Tunis: Tunis, Ariana, Ben Arous and La Manouba.
